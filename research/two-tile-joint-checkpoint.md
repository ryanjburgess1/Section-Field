# Two-tile joint checkpoint

This checkpoint tests connections between confirmed pilot vectors before returning to larger sections.

The search varies tile order, quarter-turn rotation, mirroring, vertical offset, scale, and a small horizontal overlap. A joint is rejected unless:

- The overlap is between 2.5% and 28% of the smaller mass.
- A connected mass component contains substantial geometry from both tiles.
- One supported void crosses the seam, meets both tile masses, and retains a portion of each tile's own void.
- The void has enough area, span, and clearance to be more than a hairline artifact.

Ranking considers mass continuity, balanced contact with both tiles, a mix of broad and narrow spatial widths, horizontal span, and void area. The result is still a geometric proposal, not a verified usable building section or room typology. Review the joints for convincing spatial hierarchy and opportunities for further assembly before expanding to three or more tiles.
