# Joint variation checkpoint

The project record now holds reviewed two-tile studies with stable IDs, source seed and original study number, full-precision poses, vertical reflection, ratings, tags, and notes. The saved reference studies are:

- Study 02: tiles 02 and 04, rated strong for clear horizontal platforms and connected spaces.
- Study 15 original: tiles 25 and 29, rated weak in its original orientation.
- Study 15 flipped: the whole arrangement reflected top to bottom, awaiting review.

Each strong or flipped reference can produce eight named, repeatable nearby variations: closer or looser overlap, higher or lower placement, rotation in either direction, and modest scale changes. The reflected reference applies vertical and angular changes in the displayed direction. New variations retain their parent ID and can be rated independently. Geometry checks flag changes that lose the tested mass and void connection.

This is a comparison checkpoint. A strong rating is the user's architectural assessment; a passing geometry check is only a necessary technical condition. The current project record stores poses and tile IDs but does not snapshot the vector geometry, so editing an approved source vector later will change the rendered appearance of its studies.
